Your music, your privacy.

LYRICS is an offline-first studio. Import audio from your device, write or paste your lyrics, and sync each line while the track plays. Your projects are saved in this browser using local storage technology.

Tip: Select a lyric line and press the space bar while the song plays to timestamp it. Export an .LRC file to use synchronized lyrics in compatible players.
